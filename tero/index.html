How to play

1

Tap to launch

Shoot your comet up into a sky full of stars.

2

Hold to orbit

Tether to the nearest star and swing around it. Grab it inside its golden ring for a PERFECT catch.

3

Release to fling

Let go at the right moment and fly to the next star. Keep climbing, the bottom of the screen is rising.

Features

  • One-thumb controls. Tap, hold, release. That's the whole game, and it never gets old.
  • Combos. Perfect catches chain together for big points, and the chime rises with every link.
  • Danger and daring. Stars burn out if you orbit too long. Red spike mines kill on contact, but grazing past one earns a CLOSE bonus.
  • Boosters. Shield, magnet, anchor and double points help you push further.
  • Trail skins. Collect shards ◆ to unlock Ember, Venom, Nova, Aurum and Prism.
  • Daily rewards and online leaderboards with Google Play Games.
