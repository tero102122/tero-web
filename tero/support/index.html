Tero Support

Help for Tero on iPhone and Android

Questions, bugs or purchase problems? Email tero102122@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days. Please include your device model and what happened.

How to play

  • Tap to launch from the first star.
  • Hold to tether to the nearest star and orbit it. Release to fling off.
  • Grab a star while you're inside its golden ring for a PERFECT catch. Perfect catches in a row build a combo.
  • The screen keeps rising and the bottom ends your run. Orbit too long and the star burns out. Avoid the red spike mines.
  • Collect shards to unlock and upgrade skins. Each skin has its own ability.

I bought something and didn't get it

Open the SHOP and tap Restore purchases at the bottom (iPhone). On Android, purchases are restored automatically when you open the game. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of the purchase.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your device only. It's deleted if you uninstall the game, and it doesn't move between iPhone and Android. Booster Pass and Starter Pack purchases come back with Restore purchases. Shards and boosters you bought as consumables can't be restored.

Ads won't load

Ads are optional and only play when you tap a button for a reward. If a button says "AD LOADING", check your internet connection and try again in a minute.

Privacy

Read our privacy policy. You can change ad privacy choices from the Privacy link on the game's main menu, and on iPhone in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
