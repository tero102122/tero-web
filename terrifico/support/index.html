Terrifico Support

Help for Terrifico on iPhone and Android

Questions, bugs or purchase problems? Email tero102122@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days. Please include your device model, the level number and what happened.

How to play

  • Cars drive themselves. Each one must reach the garage of its own color without crashing.
  • Cars go straight when they can. At a T junction they turn right, and at a dead end they turn around.
  • Pick a sign in the tray at the bottom, then tap a road tile to place it. Tap a placed arrow to turn it; tap it once it has pointed every way (or tap a stop sign) to remove it.
  • Arrows steer every car at a junction. Colored arrows only steer cars of that color. Stop signs hold a car for one beat, to fix the timing at a crossing.
  • Press GO to watch your plan. Press STOP to go back and change it.
  • Solve a level with the fewest signs (its par) for three stars.

I'm stuck on a level

Tap the 💡 button for a hint: a glowing sign shows where the next one goes. The 👁 button shows the route every car will take. Both cost coins, which you collect on the road, from the daily gift, or by watching an optional video. With the No-Ads Pass or the Everything Bundle, hints and route lines are free.

I bought something and didn't get it

Open ✨ Extras on the home screen and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you bought with. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of the purchase.

I still see ads after buying

Any purchase (the No-Ads Pass, a pack or the bundle) removes the ads between levels. Tap Restore purchases in Extras and they should stop. The optional videos for coins stay available, but only play when you tap them.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history. A refunded pack locks again, and the ads come back if you own nothing else.

My progress is gone

Progress (stars, coins, records and Workshop levels) is saved on your device only. It's deleted if you uninstall the game, and it doesn't move between iPhone and Android. Purchases come back with Restore purchases.

Level codes

With the Level Workshop pack you can build levels and share them as a code. Anyone can play a code for free: open Extras → Level Workshop → "Got a level code? Play it for free" and paste it.

Ads won't load

If a video button says it is still loading, check your internet connection and try again in a minute. The game itself plays offline.

Privacy

Read our privacy policy. You can change ad privacy choices from the Privacy link on the game's home screen, and on iPhone in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
