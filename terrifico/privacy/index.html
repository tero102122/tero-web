Privacy Policy for Terrifico

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Terrifico (the "App", Android package com.terogames.terrifico) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App is published by TerO Games ("we", "us").

In short: we do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and we do not run our own servers. Your game progress stays on your device. The App shows ads and offers optional one-time purchases that add extra content; buying any of them also removes the ads between levels. Ads and purchases work through Google services, which process some device data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device only: completed levels, stars, coins, settings (sound, music), your daily-gift date, simple ad counters (how many levels you have finished since the last ad), which optional purchases you own, the vehicle you picked in the garage and any levels you build, generate or import in the Level Workshop. We never receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its storage. If Android's backup is enabled on your device, it may be included in your personal Google backup. That backup is controlled by Google and your account settings, not by us.

The Level Workshop can copy a level code to your clipboard. The code holds only that level's layout, name and sign counts. The App does not send it anywhere: you choose whether and where to paste it. Anyone can paste a level code into the App and play that level, with or without a purchase.

"AI Generated" levels are built on your device by the App's own puzzle generator. Nothing is sent to an online service to make them, and they are not based on any information about you.

3. Advertising

The App shows a full-screen ad between levels, roughly once every two levels you complete. Ads are never shown during the first tutorial levels, never while you are solving a puzzle, and never twice within a short time. The App also offers optional rewarded videos (for example, to earn coins for a hint or to double a level reward); these only play when you tap a button to watch one. The App has no banner ads.

Any purchase in the App (the No-Ads Pass, an extra content pack or the bundle) permanently turns off the between-level ads. The optional rewarded videos stay available. If a purchase is refunded and you own no other, the ads return.

Ads may be personalized based on your Advertising ID. If you are in a region that requires consent, the App asks before showing personalized ads. You can change that choice at any time from Privacy → Ad privacy choices on the App's home screen. On any device you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. In-app purchases

The App offers optional one-time purchases: the No-Ads Pass, extra content packs (100 new levels, a level editor with generated levels, and vehicle styles), and a bundle that includes all of them. The free game can be played to the end without buying anything.

Purchases are processed by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service. We never see or store your card or payment details. Refunds are handled through Google Play.

5. How we use information

  • To run the game and keep your progress on your device.
  • To show ads, and to deliver the rewards you choose to earn by watching optional videos.
  • To unlock and restore the No-Ads Pass, the extra content packs and the bundle if you buy them.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not collect information to build profiles about you ourselves.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data on our own systems. Local game data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage in Android settings. Data held by Google services is retained under Google's policies. You can manage it at myaccount.google.com. For any privacy request, contact us at the address below.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store your personal information ourselves, most requests are fulfilled through the controls above or through Google. We will still help with any request you send us.

9. Security

Game data stays in the App's private storage on your device. All communication with Google services uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the App gains new features. The updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
