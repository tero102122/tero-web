Rico's Safari Salon Support

Help for Rico's Safari Salon on iPhone, iPad and Android

Questions, ideas or something not working? Email tero102122@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days. Please include your device model and what happened.

How to play

  • Tap PLAY on the home screen. A muddy animal arrives at the salon.
  • Pick a treatment: Bath, Facial, Manicure, Pedicure, Makeup, Perfume or Dress Up. Follow the pointing hand: tap the tools and rub on the animal.
  • When the animal looks fabulous, tap Photo! to finish the makeover. You earn up to three stars, coins and experience, and the photo goes into the Album.
  • Each level-up welcomes a new animal friend, up to twelve.
  • Spend coins on new hats, glasses, necklaces and outfits in Dress Up.

How do I get more coins?

Finish makeovers (more stars give more coins), level up, and collect the Daily Gift on the home screen every day. You can also tap Free Coins to watch a short optional video. There are no purchases in the game: coins can't be bought with real money.

Videos won't load

Videos are optional and only play when you tap to watch one. If none is available, check the internet connection and try again in a few minutes. Sometimes there simply isn't a video to show, and the game works fine without them.

Sound or music

Open Settings on the home screen to turn sound effects and music on or off. On iPhone and iPad, also check that the silent switch or Silent Mode is off and the volume is up.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on the device only. It's deleted if the game is uninstalled or its storage is cleared, and it doesn't move between devices. It can come back from a device backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad, Google backup on Android) if one was made while the game was installed.

For parents

  • No accounts, no chat, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.
  • Ads are child-directed and not personalized, and only play when your child chooses to watch one for coins. To stop videos loading at all, turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data while playing. The game works offline.
  • Read the full privacy policy.
