Privacy Policy for Rico's Safari Salon

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Rico's Safari Salon (the "App", on Android, iPhone and iPad as com.terogames.ricosafarisalon) collects, how it is used, and the choices parents have. The App is published by TerO Games ("we", "us"). The App is designed for children.

In short:

  • We do not ask for or collect a name, email address, photos, contacts, precise location or any other personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no chat, no sign-in and no in-app purchases.
  • Game progress is saved only on the device. We do not run our own servers.
  • The App shows optional video ads that a player chooses to watch to earn in-game coins. These ads come from Google AdMob. They are requested as child-directed, they are not personalized, and the App does not use the device's advertising ID.

1. Information stored on the device

The App saves game progress locally on the device only: level and experience points, in-game coins, unlocked outfits, the photo album of finished makeovers (drawings made inside the App, not camera photos), the daily-gift streak, how many reward videos were watched today, the animal currently in the salon, and sound and music settings. We never receive this data. It is deleted when the App is uninstalled or its storage is cleared. If the device's backup is turned on (Google backup on Android, iCloud Backup on iPhone and iPad), this data may be included in the device owner's backup. That backup is controlled by Google or Apple and the account settings, not by us.

2. Information processed by third-party services

The App uses one third-party service, which may process information under its own privacy policy:

ServiceWhat it is used forData it may process
Google AdMob (Android, iPhone and iPad) Optional reward videos that a player chooses to watch for in-game coins IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system information, app information, and ad interactions such as whether a video was watched to the end. Used to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. The advertising ID (Android) and the IDFA (iPhone and iPad) are not available to the App or to AdMob.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · AdMob child-directed setting (Android) · AdMob child-directed setting (iOS).

3. Advertising

Every ad in the App is optional. A video plays only when a player taps a button such as Free Coins, Watch or Double coins. The App has no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. Coins can also be earned without ads by playing (finishing makeovers, levelling up and collecting the daily gift).

All ad requests are tagged for child-directed treatment under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and for users under the age of consent in Europe, and are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences ("G"). With these settings Google does not show personalized ads or use interest-based or remarketing targeting. On Android, the App removes the advertising-ID permission and the Privacy Sandbox ad permissions. On iPhone and iPad, the App never asks for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) stays unavailable. No advertising identifier is collected.

Parents who do not want any ads to load can turn off the device's internet connection while the App is in use. The game still works offline.

4. Permissions

On Android:

  • Internet and network state: needed only to load the optional videos.
  • Vibration: small taps of haptic feedback during play.

On iPhone and iPad the App asks for no permissions; it uses the internet only to load the optional videos.

The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or files, and does not ask for permission to track.

5. How we use information

  • To run the game and keep progress on the device.
  • To give the coins a player earns by choosing to watch a video.

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not build profiles about players.

6. Children

The App is made for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (or anyone else), and the App has no way for a player to enter or share it. The only data that leaves the device is the technical information Google AdMob needs to deliver child-directed, non-personalized ads, as described above.

Parents or guardians can contact us at any time with questions or requests, using the address below.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any data on our own systems. Game data on the device is removed by uninstalling the App or by clearing its storage in Android settings, or on any device by using Settings → Reset progress inside the App. Data processed by Google is retained under Google's policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, COPPA in the U.S., or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store personal information ourselves, most requests are handled through the controls above or through Google. We will still help with any request you send us.

9. Security

Game data stays in the App's private storage on the device. Communication with Google's ad services uses encrypted connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the App gains new features. The updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
